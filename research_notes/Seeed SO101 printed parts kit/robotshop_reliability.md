## 1. Is the listing stocked in the US, or on-demand/drop-shipped from Seeed? What lead times are stated?

### Takeaway

## 3. Cancellation, refund and return policies

### Takeaway
RobotShop's standard return policy (as shown on product pages) requires a Return Authorization number, unused items in original packaging, and customer-paid return shipping unless the item is defective or RobotShop made a shipping error. Shipping fees aren't refunded, and refunds go out within 7 days of inspection. A 20% restocking fee can apply to large-quantity returns of special-order / non-standard-inventory items. I couldn't find a published cancellation policy or damage-reporting window.

### Cited Findings
Return policy text, via search summaries of RobotShop product pages carrying the standard policy block ([example 1](https://www.robotshop.com/products/mts-tooth-10pk), [example 2](https://www.robotshop.com/products/charge-lead-banana-19-in-1), [example 3](https://www.robotshop.com/products/caster-wheel-8mm-diameter)):
- Returns need a Return Authorization (RA) number obtained beforehand. Unauthorized returns are refused, and the RA expires after 30 days.
- Items must be unused, unmodified, in original packaging.
- Shipping fees are non-refundable. The customer pays return shipping unless the product is confirmed defective or RobotShop made a verified shipping error.
- Refund issued within 7 days after RobotShop inspects the return.
- "A 20% restocking fee may apply" to large-quantity returns of items ordered specifically for a customer or not part of standard inventory.
- Remedies for manufacturing defects fall under warranty and are at the vendor's discretion.

From [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/robotshop.com):
- One 2026 reviewer says RobotShop attempted a $35 restocking fee, then waived it and issued a full refund (€240.24) after escalation (Jun/Jul 2026).
- Another got a refund, but a $120 return-shipping cost wasn't covered (May 2026, marketplace item).
- One Jul 2026 reviewer praised a quick, professional order cancellation.
- RobotShop replies to 100% of negative reviews, typically within 24 h.

### Inferences
- If the parts arrive damaged or incomplete, it should count as a shipping error or defect, so RobotShop pays the return. Remedies run through RA and inspection, so a replacement would probably take 1–3+ weeks, likely past Nov 15. Photograph the box and parts on arrival and report immediately.
- Cancelling before shipment appears possible in practice (one positive review), but I found no written guarantee. On-demand or special-order items may be harder to cancel once RobotShop has placed the supplier order.
- The financial downside is small: ~$55 total, and at worst you lose non-refundable shipping (~$16.57) plus return postage.

### Gaps
- Couldn't access RobotShop's official return-policy, cancellation or shipping-policy pages (HTTP 403). Policy details above are search-summary paraphrases and should be confirmed on the live site.
- No stated time window for reporting damaged or missing parts.
- No written cancellation policy for on-demand or special-order items.
